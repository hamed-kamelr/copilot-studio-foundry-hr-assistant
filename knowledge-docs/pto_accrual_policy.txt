PAID TIME OFF (PTO) ACCRUAL POLICY (SAMPLE)
Effective January 1, 2026

1. ACCRUAL RATES BY TENURE
PTO accrues monthly based on an employee's length of service, measured from their hire date:
- 0 to 2 years of service: 1.25 days per month (15 days per year)
- 3 to 5 years of service: 1.67 days per month (20 days per year)
- Over 5 years of service: 2.08 days per month (25 days per year)

2. HOW ACCRUAL WORKS
PTO accrues on the last day of each completed calendar month of employment. An employee
hired mid-month begins accruing from the following full month. Accrual is prorated only in
an employee's final partial month of employment upon separation, calculated as
(days worked in the month / total days in the month) x monthly accrual rate.

3. CARRYOVER
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any
balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31 unless local law requires otherwise.

4. CALCULATING A FUTURE BALANCE
To estimate PTO balance on a future date:
Projected balance = current balance + (number of full months remaining until that date x
applicable monthly accrual rate) - any PTO already scheduled to be used in that period.
Tenure-based rate changes take effect the month following the employee's service
anniversary date.
